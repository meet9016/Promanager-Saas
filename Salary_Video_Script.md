# Promanager SaaS - Salary / Payroll Management Video Script

**Video Title:** Promanager SaaS me Salary aur Payroll Kaise Manage Karein
**Target Audience:** Admin, HR, and Managers using the Promanager SaaS Application.

---

## 1. Introduction (0:00 - 0:30)
**Visuals:** Start on the main dashboard, then click on the 'Payroll' ya 'Salary' module from the left sidebar navigation.
**Voiceover (Script):**
"Hello everyone! Aaj ke is video mein hum dekhenge ki Promanager SaaS application mein 'Salary' yaani 'Payroll' ko kaise process kiya jata hai. Month-end par har company ke liye salary calculate karna ek lamba process hota hai, lekin is software ki madad se aap automatically attendance ke basis par accurately salary generate aur finalize kar sakte hain. Chaliye dekhte hain kaise."

---

## 2. Monthly Payroll (0:30 - 1:30)
**Visuals:** Navigate to the `Monthly Payroll` section. Select a month and year from the dropdown and show the list of employees with their calculated salaries.
**Voiceover (Script):**
"Sabse pehle hum 'Monthly Payroll' section mein jayenge. 
- Yahan aapko bas specific Month aur Year select karna hai.
- System automatically har employee ki us mahine ki attendance (present days, leaves, late marks) check karta hai aur unki salary calculate kar deta hai.
- Is list mein aap har employee ka Basic Salary, Allowances, aur Deductions ek hi screen par details mein dekh sakte hain. Isse calculation mein koi error nahi hoti."

---

## 3. Finalize Payroll & Salary Slips (1:30 - 2:30)
**Visuals:** Move to the `Finalize Payroll` section. Show how you can review the final amounts, click on finalize/approve, and optionally demonstrate a salary slip.
**Voiceover (Script):**
"Salary calculate hone ke baad, final step hota hai use approve ya finalize karna. Iske liye 'Finalize Payroll' page par click karein.
- Yahan par HR ya Admin final amounts ko review kar sakte hain. Agar kisi ka koi adjustment ya bonus baki hai, toh use check kiya ja sakta hai.
- Ek baar jab aap saari details se satisfied hon, toh aap is month ke payroll ko 'Finalize' kar sakte hain.
- Finalize hone ke baad, system employees ki Salary Slips automatically generate kar deta hai. Employees apne portal se is payslip ko directly download ya view kar sakte hain."

---

## 4. Conclusion / Outro (2:30 - 3:00)
**Visuals:** Return to the Dashboard. Show support info or company logo.
**Voiceover (Script):**
"Toh dosto, yeh tha Promanager SaaS mein Monthly Salary aur Payroll manage karne ka aasan tarika. Is process ke through aapka kaafi time bachega aur calculations hamesha accurate rahengi.
Agar aapke paas koi bhi sawal hain toh aap hamari support team se jud sakte hain. Video dekhne ke liye dhanyawad, aur channel ko subscribe zaroor karein!"

--- 
**Note:** Aap is script ko apni screen ke hisaab se thoda bahut customize kar sakte hain.
