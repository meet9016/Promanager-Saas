# PagarPe SaaS - Shift Management Video Script

**Video Title:** PagarPe SaaS me Shift Management Kaise Karein
**Target Audience:** Admin, HR, and Managers using the PagarPe SaaS Application.

---

## 1. Introduction (0:00 - 0:30)
**Visuals:** Start on the main dashboard, then click on the 'Shift Management' module from the left sidebar navigation.
**Voiceover (Script):**
"Hello everyone! Aaj ke is video mein hum dekhenge ki PagarPe SaaS application mein 'Shift Management' ko kaise use kiya jata hai. Agar aapki company mein alag-alag shifts (jaise morning, evening, night shift) mein kaam hota hai, toh yeh eature aapke liye kaafi useful hai. Is module ki madad se aap aasani se nayi shift create kar sakte hain, employees ko shift assign kar sakte hain, aur zaroorat padne par unki shift reallocate bhi kar sakte hain."
- Toh chaliye, bina kisi deri ke Shift Management ko step-by-step samajhte hain.

---

## 2. Create Shift (0:30 - 1:15)
**Visuals:** Click on the `Create Shift` option or button. Show the form where shift details are entered (Shift Name, Start Time, End Time, Grace Period, etc.).
**Voiceover (Script):**
"Sabse pehle, humein system mein apne office ki shifts set up karni hoti hain. Iske liye 'Create Shift' par click karein.
- Yahan aap nayi shift ka name daal sakte hain (jaise Morning Shift).
- Uske baad shift ka (Start Time) aur (End Time) set karein.
- Agar aap apne employees ko aane mein kuch minutes ki chhoot (Grace Period) dena chahte hain, toh woh bhi aap yahan configure kar sakte hain. 
- Ek din ki shift timing set karne ke baad, aap usi timing ko baaki required days ke liye copy bhi kar sakte hain.
- Details check karke 'Save' kar dein. Aapki nayi shift list mein add ho jayegi."

---

## 3. Assign Shift (1:15 - 2:00)
**Visuals:** Navigate to the `Assign Shift` page. Show how to select a shift and select the employees or departments to assign it to.
**Voiceover (Script):**
"Shifts create karne ke baad, agla step hai unhe employees ko assign karna. Iske liye hum 'Assign Shift' section mein jayenge.
- employees ya poore department ko select kar sakte hain jinhe yeh shift deni hai.
- Yahan se aap koi bhi banayi hui shift select kar sakte hain.
- employees ki attendance is nayi shift timing ke according track hogi."

---

## 4. Shift Reallocation (2:00 - 2:45)
**Visuals:** Go to the `Shift Reallocation` section. Demonstrate how to change an existing shift for a specific employee.
**Voiceover (Script):**
- Kabhi-kabhi kisi employee ki shift beech mahine mein change karni pad sakti hai. Aisi situation ke liye PagarPe mein Shift Reallocation ka feature diya gaya hai.
- Iske liye sabse pehle Shift Reallocation section mein jayenge.Yahan sabse pehle From Shift select karein, yani employee ki current shift.
- Uske baad To Shift select karein, yani employee ko jo nayi shift assign karni hai. Ab jis date se shift change karni hai, woh applicable date select karein.
- uske baad un employees ko select karein, jinki shift change karni hai.
- Sabhi details ek baar check karne ke baad Confirm reallocation par click karein.
- Jaise hi update complete hoga, selected employees ka naya shift schedule automatically system mein apply ho jayega.
- Is tarah, aap employees ki shift ko easily reallocate aur manage kar sakte hain.

---

## 5. Conclusion / Outro (2:45 - 3:15)
**Visuals:** Return to the Shift Management dashboard or main screen. Show support email/number on screen.
**Voiceover (Script):**
"Toh dosto, yeh tha PagarPe mein Shift Management ka poora process. Is feature se aap easily multi-shift environment ko control aur track kar sakte hain.
Agar aapko koi query ho toh aap hamari support team se contact kar sakte hain. Video informative laga ho toh like karein aur channel ko subscribe zaroor karein! Milte hain next tutorial mein."

--- 
**Note:** Aap is script ko apni screen layout aur buttons ke hisaab se customize karke voiceover record kar sakte hain.
