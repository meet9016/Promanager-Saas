# Promanager SaaS - Leave and Holiday Management Video Script

**Video Title:** Promanager SaaS me Leaves aur Holidays Kaise Manage Karein
**Target Audience:** Admin, HR, and Managers using the Promanager SaaS Application.

---

## 1. Introduction (0:00 - 0:30)
**Visuals:** Start on the main dashboard, then click on the 'Leave' ya 'Leave Management' module from the left sidebar navigation.
**Voiceover (Script):**
"Hello everyone! Aaj hum dekhenge ki Promanager SaaS application mein 'Leave and Holiday Management' module ko kaise use kiya jata hai. Kisi bhi company mein holidays set karna aur employees ki chhuttiyan (leaves) track karna ek zaroori hissa hota hai. Is software ke through aap company holidays add kar sakte hain, aur employees easily leave apply kar sakte hain jisko HR turant approve ya reject kar sakte hain. Chaliye dekhte hain."

---

## 2. Holiday Setup (0:30 - 1:15)
**Visuals:** Click on the `Holiday` section. Show how to add a new holiday (Date, Name/Occasion, etc.) and view the yearly holiday list.
**Voiceover (Script):**
"Sabse pehle aate hain 'Holidays' par. 
- Yahan aap apni company ka poore saal ka holiday calendar set up kar sakte hain. 
- Naya holiday add karne ke liye date select karein aur festival ya occasion ka naam daalkar save kar dein. 
- Yeh holidays automatically sabhi employees ke dashboard aur attendance mein reflect ho jate hain, jisse mahine ki salary calculate karte waqt in dinon ko automatically 'Paid Holiday' maan liya jata hai."

---

## 3. Leave Application (1:15 - 2:00)
**Visuals:** Navigate to the `Leave Application` page. Demonstrate filling out the leave form (Leave Type, From Date, To Date, Reason).
**Voiceover (Script):**
"Ab baat karte hain Leaves ki. Agar kisi employee ko chhutti chahiye, toh woh application se 'Leave Application' kar sakte hain.
- Yahan unhein sirf ek chhota sa form bharna hota hai.
- Sabse pehle 'Leave Type' select karein (jaise Sick Leave, Casual Leave, ya Paid Leave).
- Phir Date select karein ki kab se kab tak chhutti chahiye, aur ek valid reason likh kar submit kar dein. 

---

## 4. Leave Status & Approvals (2:00 - 2:45)
**Visuals:** Move to the `Leave Status` section (logged in as Admin/HR). Show the list of pending leaves, and demonstrate approving/rejecting a leave request.
**Voiceover (Script):**
"Employee ke leave apply karte hi, HR ya Admin ke paas request chali jati hai, jise woh 'Leave Status' section mein dekh sakte hain.
- Is page par sabhi employees ki Pending, Approved, aur Rejected leaves ki list show hoti hai.
- HR yahan se request review kar sakte hain. Agar sab theek hai toh 'Approve' par click karein, warna reason dekar 'Reject' kar sakte hain.
- Approve hote hi employee ke leave balance se chhutti deduct ho jati hai, aur iska asar automatically unki attendance par padta hai."

---

## 5. Conclusion / Outro (2:45 - 3:15)
**Visuals:** Return to the Dashboard. Display support email or contact number.
**Voiceover (Script):**
"Toh dosto, yeh tha Promanager SaaS mein Leaves aur Holidays ko manage karne ka asaan tarika. Is module ki madad se leave tracking completely transparent aur paperless ban jati hai.
Agar aapka koi sawaal ho toh comment karein ya support team se contact karein. Video dekhne ke liye shukriya, aur channel ko subscribe karna na bhoolein!"

--- 
**Note:** Aap is script ko apni UI screens aur buttons ke hisaab se customize karke apna voiceover de sakte hain.
