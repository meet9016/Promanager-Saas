# Promanager SaaS - Attendance Module Video Script

**Video Title:** Promanager SaaS me Attendance Manage Kaise Karein (Daily & Monthly)
**Target Audience:** Admin, Managers, and Employees using the Promanager SaaS Application.

---

## 1. Introduction (0:00 - 0:30)
**Visuals:** Show the main dashboard of Promanager SaaS, then navigate to the 'Attendance' section in the side menu.
**Voiceover (Script):**
"Hello everyone! Aaj ke is video mein hum dekhenge ki Promanager SaaS application mein Attendance module ko kaise use kiya jata hai. Is software mein attendance ko manage karna bahut hi simple aur effective banaya gaya hai. Hamare paas yahan do main sections hain: Daily Attendance aur Monthly Attendance. Toh chaliye start karte hain Daily Attendance se."

---

## 2. Daily Attendance (0:30 - 1:45)
**Visuals:** Click on the `Daily Attendance` page. Show the list of employees, their punch-in/punch-out status, and the current day's record. Hover over key metrics.
**Voiceover (Script):**
"Daily Attendance page par aate hi, aapko aaj ki date ka poora attendance record mil jayega. 
- Yahan aap dekh sakte hain ki kaun se employees present hain, kaun absent hain, aur kisne half-day liya hai.
- Is list mein har employee ka Punch-In time aur Punch-Out time clearly track hota hai.
- Agar koi employee late aata hai, toh system use automatic highlight kar deta hai, jisse managers ko tracking mein asani hoti hai.
- Aap date filter ka use karke kisi pichle din ki attendance bhi check kar sakte hain, aur kisi bhi employee ka daily status update ya modify bhi kar sakte hain agar aapke paas admin rights hain."

---

## 3. Monthly Attendance (1:45 - 3:00)
**Visuals:** Navigate to the `Monthly Attendance` page from the side menu. Show the tabular view representing the entire month's dates as columns and employees as rows. 
**Voiceover (Script):**
"Ab aate hain Monthly Attendance par. Ye page HRs aur Managers ke liye bahut useful hai kyunki yahan poore mahine ki report ek hi screen par mil jati hai.
- Jaise ki aap dekh rahe hain, is page par humein har employee ka poore mahine ka attendance calendar dikhta hai.
- Yahan par Total Present Days, Absent Days, Leaves, aur Late days ka total automatically calculate ho jata hai.
- Is summary se payroll process karna kafi aasan ho jata hai.
- Aap easily dropdown se specific month ya year select kar sakte hain, aur sath hi is report ko export bhi kar sakte hain taaki future reference ke liye aapke paas record rahe."

---

## 4. Conclusion / Outro (3:00 - 3:30)
**Visuals:** Go back to the Dashboard or show a generic summary view. Display contact/support email on the screen.
**Voiceover (Script):**
"Toh dosto, yeh tha Promanager SaaS ka Daily aur Monthly Attendance overview. Is feature ki madad se aap apni company ki attendance tracking ko 100% digital aur error-free bana sakte hain.
Agar aapko koi query ho toh aap hamari support team se contact kar sakte hain. Video dekhne ke liye shukriya, channel ko subscribe zaroor karein!"
